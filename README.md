# 🚀 AI SRE Copilot — Autonomous Incident Intelligence Platform

AI SRE Copilot is an AI-powered incident intelligence platform designed to simulate modern enterprise SRE workflows with real-time monitoring, AI-driven Root Cause Analysis (RCA), intelligent incident routing, recovery workflows, executive reporting, and observability integrations.

The platform demonstrates how AI can reduce Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR) by automating incident detection, classification, routing, recovery recommendations, and operational decision-making during production outages.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

## AI Incident Root Cause Analyzer for SRE Teams

During production outages, engineers waste valuable time switching between logs, dashboards, alerts, traces, and monitoring systems to identify the actual root cause.

This delay increases:
- Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR)
- Downtime
- SLA breach risk
- Revenue impact
- Operational overhead

AI SRE Copilot solves this problem by acting as an AI-powered incident intelligence agent that simulates integrations with observability platforms like Datadog, Grafana, and New Relic.

The platform analyzes logs, telemetry, metrics, traces, incidents, and service health in real time to identify probable root causes and suggest fixes instantly.

---

# ✅ Our Solution

AI SRE Copilot is an autonomous incident intelligence platform that helps SRE teams detect, analyze, route, and recover from infrastructure incidents faster.

The system:
- Connects simulated Datadog, Grafana, and New Relic telemetry
- Analyzes logs and incidents in real time
- Identifies probable root causes
- Classifies incident priority
- Assigns incidents intelligently
- Suggests recovery workflows
- Activates emergency war rooms
- Generates executive incident reports
